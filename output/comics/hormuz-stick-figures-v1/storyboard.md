# Hormuz: six-page stick-figure comic

Built-in image generation was used for all six pages. The prompts are saved in `prompts/`.

Source: [ClearFrame report snapshot](source-report.html), copied from the workspace's `results-preview.html`.
The dialogue is reconstructed from the report, not authenticated quotations. Stick figures represent analytical roles; their conversations are not depictions of actual meetings.

The series covers all five findings: agency attribution (page 1), presuppositions and doctrine (page 2), selective criteria (page 3), suppressed alternatives (page 4), and attention to suffering (page 5). Page 6 brings them together without inventing a resolution.

The cast includes the U.S., Iran, Israel, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Michael Singh and his former Bush-administration role, Trump's administration, Congress, Republican lawmakers, American voters and families, families elsewhere, farmers, an Iranian military commander, the U.N. warning cited in the comparison, and the International Maritime Organization. Publications are distinguished from government actors. Articles listed in the report's library that do not contribute to its findings are not expanded into additional story claims.
